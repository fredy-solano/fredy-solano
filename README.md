## How I work

- Risk first. Expected outcome is the last input, not the first.
- Portfolios are interacting risk factors, not lists of securities.
- Every claim is tagged as fact, assumption, estimate or inference, and carries its source.
- Decisions are recorded before they are executed and reviewed after they close.
- Independent challengers read the record, never the reasoning that produced it.
